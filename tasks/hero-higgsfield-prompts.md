## Pick rules (apply to every plate)

- The centre stays plain dark wall. Anything sitting where the gauge goes = reject.
- One lamp, upper-left. Light from anywhere else = reject.
- No red lights, LEDs or neon anywhere. The page's one race-red is the sweep under the headline.
- No text, logos, brand names, people, cars.
- Dark overall. If the plate is bright, the headline loses contrast and the smoke can't read through.
- Materials read real: rust, worn paint, brass, walnut. If it looks rendered or clean, reject.
